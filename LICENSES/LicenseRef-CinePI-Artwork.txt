publish a formal license for these visuals at the time of this fork.

UAB Kurokesu uses them in good faith as a contributing fork that
builds on the upstream CinePI software stack. Kurokesu does not claim
the underlying CinePI artwork and welcomes contact from the project's
maintainers to clarify or formalize permission.
